05 — No rushed switch

We do the heavy lifting, without touching court time.

Moving members, memberships and payments is a big step. Both systems run side by side until Egret is comfortable.

Step 1

Free pilot

A small group of members books and signs up through the app while everyone else stays on MemberMe+.

Step 2

Data migration

We import members, plan types and expiry dates from EZFacility so nobody re-registers.

Step 3

Side by side

Both systems run in parallel. Renewals move over as they come due, not all at once.

Step 4

Member onboarding

Front-desk QR signs, a short how-to, and in-person help at open play.

Want to see it on a real phone?

A 15-minute demo at the club, or a free pilot with a small group of members. Whichever is easier.
