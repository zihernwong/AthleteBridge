# AthleteBridge Feature Roadmap

## In Progress
- **Event Signups** — SignUpGenius-style event creation with public web signup page (plan ready)

---

## High Impact — Community Growth

### Match Challenge System
Players challenge each other to matches at connected venues. Combines Players to Play With + Places to Play into an actionable flow. Challenged player accepts/declines, match auto-creates a booking or calendar event.

### Club/Group System
Create badminton clubs with member lists, group chats, and recurring sessions. Club admins manage membership. Big retention driver — gives users a "home" in the app.

### Social Feed
Community posts for match results, event recaps, achievements, and general updates. Keeps users opening the app between sessions. Could include photo/video sharing for highlights and technique clips.

---

## High Impact — Coach Revenue

### Session Notes & Progress Tracking
Coaches write post-session feedback visible to the client. Clients track improvement over time with a timeline view. Differentiator from generic booking apps — adds ongoing value beyond scheduling.

### Recurring Bookings
Weekly/biweekly auto-repeat for coaching sessions. Reduces friction for regulars who book the same coach at the same time every week. Auto-creates bookings with one-tap confirm.

### Coach Analytics Dashboard
Revenue trends, client retention rates, popular time slots, booking conversion rate. Helps coaches optimize their business. Could be a Coach Pro subscription perk.

---

## Medium Impact — Platform Completeness

### Waitlist for Full Events
When signup events fill up, allow waitlist signups with automatic promotion when spots open. Sends notification when promoted from waitlist.

### Equipment Marketplace
Buy/sell used rackets, shoes, shuttlecocks within the community. Listings with photos, price, condition. Message seller to coordinate. Natural extension of the stringing service.

### Court Reservation
Book specific courts at venues with time slots. Extends Places to Play with real-time availability. Venue contacts manage court schedules.

### In-App Payment Processing
Stripe Connect for direct payments instead of deep-linking to Venmo/PayPal. Automated payment tracking, receipts, and revenue reporting. Eliminates manual "notify coach of payment" step.
