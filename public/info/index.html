Features

Everything AthletesBridge can do

Badminton, tennis, pickleball, basketball and more.

🔎

Find & book coaches

Search the coach directory and book in a few taps.

  • Filter coaches by sport, goals, availability and rate
  • Coach profiles with bio, experience, rate range and reviews
  • Request one-on-one or group lessons
  • Hourly and total cost shown up front
  • Reschedule, cancel and book recurring sessions
  • Add confirmed bookings to your calendar
📋

Coach tools

Run your coaching business from your phone.

  • Set weekly availability and away time
  • Accept, decline or edit booking requests
  • Automatic overlap checks to prevent double booking
  • Client roster with notes and booking history
  • Session logs with metric progress tracking
  • Coach home dashboard for the day ahead
💳

Payments & insights

Know who has paid and what's coming in.

  • Track paid and unpaid status for each booking
  • Save Venmo and PayPal handles
  • Prefilled Venmo payment links
  • Revenue summaries and earnings forecasts
  • Spending forecasts for athletes
🏆

Tournaments

From local events to season-long series.

  • Browse upcoming tournaments
  • Find a tournament partner for doubles or mixed
  • Web Tournament Manager: round robin, single and double elimination
  • Singles, doubles and mixed brackets with live scores
  • Share brackets with a QR code or link
  • Season series standings and player rankings
👥

Clubs & events

Keep your club organized and engaged.

  • Create clubs and manage members and admins
  • Club announcements with push notifications
  • Sign-up events with capacity and waitlists
  • Recurring and paid events
  • Shareable sign-up links that work without the app
🏸

Racket stringing

Book your restring with a local stringer.

  • Browse stringers and their services
  • Place orders with string type and tension
  • Order timeline from drop-off to ready
  • Stringer dashboard for incoming orders
📍

Places & players

Find where to play and who to play with.

  • Places to play with photos, details and distance
  • Explore map of venues and coaches
  • Find players at your level to play with
  • Location autocomplete when booking
💬

Messaging & alerts

Stay in touch with your coach, club and partners.

  • In-app chat with photo sharing
  • Push notifications for bookings, messages and clubs
  • Tap a notification to jump straight to the right screen
  • Leave and read reviews after each session
🎯

Goals & progress

Train with a plan and see how you're improving.

  • Set training goals on your profile
  • See session logs shared by your coach
  • Metric history charts over time
  • Live session timer
